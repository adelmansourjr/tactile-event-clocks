# Stress testing tactile event clocks before spike generation

This follow-up asks whether compact tactile features survive when changes in motion or measurement alter the spikes themselves. The original strict time warp leaves event order alone by construction. Here we transform the measured pressure frames and produce fresh spikes afterwards. This distinction matters because threshold crossing depends on how a pressure trajectory is sampled.

## Data and split

Use the 7,700 raw STEMNIST pressure traces of 35 characters, with 240 readings on a 16×16 sensor at 120 Hz per trace. Validate the public archive's MD5 `6ca4638b2f95bf34f59873ab62399bd8`. Keep the original 18/6/10 train/validation/test writer split and publicly expose anonymous indices only. Training reads 4,200 traces, validation 1,575, and test 1,925. No same-writer train/test contamination. These are new perturbations of a reused corpus and writer split. They are neither fresh participants nor traces acquired at different instructed speeds. The split and all perturbation families and settings in this document are fixed before score inspection.

## Measurement model

Approximate the STEMNIST bipolar event generator by taking single-frame pressure differences above max(4, 90th percentile of nonzero magnitudes) with a strict inequality. All methods share the same pulses. A raw-pressure comparator can use the marked pressure frames before their event representation. A pressure progress clock additionally reads the full pressure signal at encoding time, with costs reported. It cannot be deployed on an event-only interface without that extra information. A separate event-only blended clock uses the pulse stream alone.

- Training and validation both include the unaltered pressure data and mild families: sinusoidal retiming (signed amplitude uniform from −0.35 to +0.35 in the derivative), random threshold quantile from 0.88 to 0.94, and independent dead taxels (5% plus a 0.125–0.21 second held-frame burst). All model fits receive the same four traces per recording, including the clean original. Training and validation use the same perturbation mechanisms and distinct component seeds.
- Final conditions are clean, piecewise retiming through a random interior knot at location 0.42–0.58 and value in either 0.15–0.25 or 0.75–0.85, coarse acquisition (one sample every third frame, interpolate then quantise to nearest multiple of 8), stricter corollary threshold (quantile 0.95–0.97), structured pressure dropouts (4 contiguous columns and a 0.30 second held-frame burst), and all four combined. These evaluated families go beyond the mild variants used for training, in either severity or type. Their definitions do not describe an actual physical acquisition. Each is shared by all methods. Seeds derive from declared split and sample index, not labels. Field manipulation is performed before differencing and thresholding.

## Features, fitting, and primary endpoint

All models use 512-dimensional square-root normalized features and the same trainable multinomial ridge readout. The event baselines are fixed absolute time, elapsed event-range time, cumulative event-rank time, concatenation of two range and two rank slices, and fully spatial pooling. Each time-aware encoding allocates events using tent weights between neighbouring slice centres on an 8×8 grid with polarity. The spatial control pools time at full 16×16 polarity resolution. A 512-dimensional direct-pressure control uses eight raw-time slices and an 8×8 grid of absolute pressure changes without polarity as an additional information-rich comparator.

Candidates use four bins with an event-only convex blend of fixed time and centred cumulative event rank, or a clock based on centred cumulative absolute pressure change before thresholding. The blend weights 0.25, 0.50, and 0.75 are fixed candidates. Model weights, feature size, four training versions per original recording, and ridge grid {0.03, 0.3, 3, 30} are matched. Select each model's ridge coefficient, strongest control, and strongest candidate by maximum worst-case equal-writer score across the four validation conditions, breaking ties by overall accuracy and then declared grid order. Score finalists and all controls on all six final conditions. No recentering, recalibrating, or threshold adjustment based on test labels or observed test results. Record raw predictions, writer means, event count ratios by condition, feature timings, and memory footprint.

The primary endpoint is the difference of the two selected methods' worst equal-writer condition accuracies among five shifted conditions. Success requires a 2-point improvement and a paired writer-bootstrap interval with lower endpoint above zero. Resample the same ten writers within every condition and method, recomputing each method's minimum in every draw. The clean accuracy difference must additionally have a two-sided interval lower bound above −2 points to meet the clean preservation gate. Single-condition comparisons and two budget-independent representation identities are descriptive. The inferential unit is the writer rather than the perturbed trace. Bootstrap precision reflects the ten observed writers only and should not be treated as a population guarantee.

## Mathematical check

Bound the distribution shift of the soft clock for the narrower case of pure deletions of a fraction of events and anchor-fixed movement of surviving timestamps. Separately prove invariance of below-threshold raw arc-length to linear subdivision of a pressure polyline, and exhibit a counterexample where nonlinear spike thresholding changes the event stream despite the same continuous path. The guarantee does not apply to arbitrary pre-encoding measurement shift in the empirical benchmark.
