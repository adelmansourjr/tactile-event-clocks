# Matched-event tactile recognition under contact displacement

The prior experiment leaves a large worst-condition accuracy gap when pressure acquisition changes. This follow-up asks whether aligning the spatial coordinate frame before voxelization improves compact classification when the same character lands elsewhere on the sensor, including cases where measurements are also missing. Centering and bounds normalization are established image-processing primitives. The contribution is a matched-input, held-out benchmark and an exact statement of what this form of alignment preserves.

Use the same 18/6/10 disjoint STEMNIST train/development/test writer partition and pressure recordings as the earlier study. They are reused across studies. All sampling, placement and loss draws have new frozen seeds. This is a stress test conditional on this labelled cohort, with no new participants or hardware. Placement transfers the recorded pressure differences to a translated taxel field and clips out-of-array offsets. Time warp and coarsening modify the pressure before computing differences. Masked taxels or intervals omit observations without inventing boundary impulses. These are controlled perturbations of observed recordings rather than new recordings or simulations of contact mechanics.

Every method sees exactly the same signed event identities and timestamps on each trace. Share a single per-trace cap of 121 largest eligible differences with deterministic random tie breaks, and discard magnitudes after selection. No method receives the transform, unavailable events, raw pressure, the condition label, the number of discarded events, or test labels. Keep all eight clock and spatial placement variants to 512 scalar features, two polarities, four temporal bins and 8-by-8 spatial support. Fit each with the same labeled views and ridge or 64-unit MLP choice grids. The neural optimizer and early stop epochs are shared. Feature costs can differ and the added centering operations are included in the scope. No hardware power or latency claim is planned.

Consider strongest fixed-frame range and rank baselines, with hard or bilinear spatial assignment, and bounding-extent-adapted range and rank baselines that are already translation invariant when no pulses are clipped. Bilinear spatial assignment is common to canonical variants and the strongest shape-alignment control. Evaluate matching variants at both model capacities. Select the baseline policy using the six development writers and the minimum of the mild shifted conditions, with ties broken by mean accuracy including clean. Pick the strongest development centroid model in the same readout class. All 16 selected within-feature/readout combinations are reported on the final set. No final retuning.

The primary gap is the difference in each policy's minimum equal-writer accuracy across placement, coarse+masked acquisition, and placement+rate+coarse+masked acquisition. The absolute improvement must be at least three points and the lower endpoint of the 12,000-draw paired writer-bootstrap interval must exceed zero. Recompute both worst conditions on every draw. Separately keep lower clean-difference endpoint strictly above −2 points. A gain requires both gates. Condition-specific estimates are descriptive. All 1,925 test recordings enter each analysis whether clipped or complete, any zero-event record maps to an all-zero feature vector, and each writer carries equal weight. The ten observed test writers and controlled shift family limit generality.

Coordinate identity: with a fixed set of observed taxel events, the numerator for centered position is N*x[i]-sum(x). A common translation cancels exactly. The same temporal/polarity voxelization then yields identical input to the fixed readout. This statement excludes translations that clip observations, new pressure dynamics, or sensor-specific response changes. Report an exact integer-offset unit test and negative controls for clipping; this theorem is a diagnostic, separate from the empirical accuracy test.

**Before final evaluation.** The initial development fit implemented only elapsed time for the bounding-extent baseline while centroid models could choose elapsed or rank. The main evaluation strengthens this control by including rank at the same capacity. Initial development hashes and scores are archived. No test computation ran for that initial version. The same archive, data splits, random seeds, endpoints, thresholds and remaining model settings carry forward.
